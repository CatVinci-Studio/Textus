## What's new

### Settings

- The settings panel is redesigned, and the desktop app and iPhone share the same one:
  - Each row has only a name and a switch or option; most of the small explanatory text is gone.
  - The writing model and the Agent model are together under "AI services › Models", sharing one "Fetch Models" button.
  - "Editor & Appearance" adds "View" (Source Mode, Typewriter Mode, Zen Mode), and "General" adds "Help" (Markdown Guide, AI Features Guide).
  - Actions such as clearing the chat history ask before they run.
- On iPhone, Settings fills the screen, categories switch along the top, and "Done" is at the top right.

### iPhone

- The menu is cut down to: Documents, New, Open, Save, Share, Find and Replace and the sidebar, and Settings.
- Opening the AI panel while editing hides the keyboard once, without flickering.
- The find bar no longer covers the menu (also fixed on Windows).
- The find bar no longer shows on the "Documents" page.

Installers for macOS, Windows and Linux are built by the release workflow.
