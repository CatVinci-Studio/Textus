## What's new

### Interface languages

- Adds Traditional Chinese, Korean, Spanish, French, German, Russian and Portuguese, for 10 interface languages in all. The help guides, the onboarding tour and the macOS menu bar are translated too.
- Language is chosen from a drop-down list, and on first launch it follows the system language.

### Levis AI

- AI settings are merged into one page, "Levis AI": connect a provider first, then set up completion, the grammar check and chat.
- Adds 18 built-in writing skills: continue writing, describe a character or scene, polish dialogue, imitate a style, expand, condense, adjust tone, remove the AI flavour, polish a translation, proofread, check consistency, write an email, official documents and notices, meeting minutes, academic polish, social media copy, suggest titles, and summarize. Type / in the chat box to use one; in Settings you can turn them off, customize them or restore the defaults.
- Typing / or @ always brings up suggestions; "、", "／" and "＠" from a Chinese input method work too.
- New "Writing conventions" setting: the grammar check checks punctuation, spacing and other details against the conventions you choose (in 10 languages).
- Replies are in the language you asked in. Suggested changes are more accurate and can be accepted whole or sentence by sentence.
- Completion reads more naturally: it no longer sticks the completion onto the previous word, and no longer makes up details.
- Grammar check fixes land in the right place and no longer change correct words next to them.
- The chat is redesigned: the input box, replies and change cards are clearer, and multi-turn chats are faster and cost less.

### Editor

- Typing the closing ** or ~~ by hand no longer messes up the formatting.
- Amounts such as $5 are no longer taken for formulas.
- Saving a file you haven't changed no longer changes its content; links and list markers stay as they were.
- Fixes the source pane not scrolling in split view, diagrams not rendering in background tabs, headings hidden after jumping from the outline, and Find not showing the first match.
- Accepting one AI change no longer invalidates the other suggestions.

### Look and feel

- Menus, dialogs, Settings, the sidebar and the AI panel have finer animations, simplified when the system's "Reduce motion" is on.
- Corner radii and spacing are consistent, and toggles in the "View" menu show a check mark on the right.
- Imported Typora themes apply fully in dark mode too, and custom styles take priority.

### Data and stability

- Settings, chat history and clipboard history are stored in local files and synced live between windows; existing data is moved over on first launch.
- Quitting quickly no longer loses settings you just changed.
- On macOS, the app stays in the Dock after you close the last window; click the icon to bring back your last documents.

Installers for macOS, Windows and Linux are built by the release workflow.
