## What's new

### iPhone

- The interface is redesigned for the way phones are used:
  - Quick Ask docks just above the keyboard and quotes the selected text; "Ask/Edit/Plan" is a segmented control.
  - Settings is a full-screen page: a grouped list, tap into each category, Back at the top left, "Done" at the top right.
  - Drop-down lists use the system picker. Long-pressing a file, a document's "⋯" and "Save changes?" open action sheets from the bottom.
  - Confirmations such as Delete use system-style alerts. Find and Replace spans the screen. The tab bar scrolls sideways, and its close buttons are easier to hit.
  - The keyboard toolbar changes with the caret: in a table it shows add row, add column and delete; with a picture selected it shows width and delete; "⋯" lists the rest. Tap a grammar underline to see the suggested fix.
  - Buttons and icons are larger, sized for fingers. The dimmed backdrop behind pop-ups reaches the status bar and the bottom of the screen.
- Quick Ask no longer disappears after you delete text.
- The AI panel can be typed in and scrolled again in long chats, and the panel's drag handle follows your finger.
- The keyboard no longer pops up and covers the AI panel after you accept a change.
- Tapping a list, code block or formula no longer shows a blue box.
- Coming back from another app no longer reloads documents that haven't changed, so an AI chat in progress is kept.

### Other

- The fix for Quick Ask disappearing after you delete text applies to the desktop app too.

Installers for macOS, Windows and Linux are built by the release workflow.
