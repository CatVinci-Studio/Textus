## What's new

Textus 1.0.2: some improvements and fixes, and the first version for iPhone and iPad.

### Community

- A new Discord community: find the link in the "Help" menu and in "Settings › General › Help".

### Editing

- The live preview of a formula no longer covers the text below a display formula.
- Zooming in source mode and split view keeps the line under the mouse in place.
- Typing `-[]` in a table cell no longer breaks the table apart.
- A to-do item with no text no longer loses its checkbox when saved.
- After a to-do is converted, spaces typed elsewhere are no longer swallowed.
- Undoing after pasting a screenshot no longer leaves a temporary picture address in the document.
- The fix-ups when an input method commits text are more reliable and no longer delete empty list items by mistake.

### iPhone and iPad

- The toolbar above the keyboard drops its Copy and Paste buttons; copy and paste use the system's long-press menu.
- Opening the sidebar closes the find bar, so the two no longer overlap.

Installers for macOS, Windows and Linux are built by the release workflow.
