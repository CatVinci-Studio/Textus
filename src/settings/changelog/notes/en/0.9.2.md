## What's new

### Fixed

- Opening the Markdown Guide or the AI Features Guide from the Help menu no longer opens several identical tabs at once.
- After you change the interface language, guides that are open and unchanged switch to the new language.
- After opening a submenu with the arrow keys in the app menu, focus moves into the submenu.

### Settings

- Options such as "Open documents in" and "Restore previous documents" are trimmed, without unneeded explanatory text. "Suggest filenames automatically" is now "Suggest a filename when saving a new file".
- In "Editor & Appearance", editing settings come first, and Custom CSS is folded into a single expandable option.

### Menus

- The View menu is regrouped: sidebar and AI panel; source, split view, typewriter and zen mode; zoom and full screen.

Installers for macOS, Windows and Linux are built by the release workflow.
