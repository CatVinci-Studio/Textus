## What's new

### Fixed

- On Linux (Wayland), the separate chat window opens again after clicking the pop-out button. Under tiling window managers such as niri, it opens as a floating window above the editing window.

### Levis AI

- The AI assistant is renamed **Levis AI**.
- Quick Ask and the chat panel on the right are separate: Quick Ask has its own short conversation that isn't written to the chat history, and Ctrl/⌘+Shift+K brings it up at the caret even while the panel is open. Click **Continue in the full conversation** at the top right of Quick Ask to hand the conversation to the panel, where it carries on and is saved to the history.
- The top of the panel is simpler: the document name and extra explanatory text are gone, and there is more space between the input box and the buttons below it.

### Interface

- Drop-down lists are drawn by the app itself, with the same corners and colours as other menus; use the up and down keys to choose, Enter to confirm and Esc to close.
- Alerts and confirmations (export failed, Pandoc needs installing, the file was changed on disk, and so on) are dialogs inside the app instead of system-style windows.
- Checkboxes, number fields and scroll bars match the rest of the interface.

Installers for macOS, Windows and Linux are built by the release workflow.
