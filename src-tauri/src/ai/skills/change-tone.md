# Change tone

## Scope
- Only the passage named (selection, named passage, or current section). Don't spread the change to neighbouring text; offer that in the reply.
- With no target tone given, ask once which tone, offering three or four choices.

## Do
- Change register, not content: the same facts, commitments, dates, names and requests.
- Keep one register throughout the passage: no mixing です・ます and である, 하십시오체 and 해요체, 您 and 你, Sie and du, vous and tu, usted and tú.
- Formal is not wordy: no inflated vocabulary or stacked courtesy phrases.
- Japanese: avoid 二重敬語 except the fixed forms; させていただく only where permission and benefit are real; ウチ・ソト for names and titles.
- Korean: no 사물 높임 (커피 나오셨습니다); no 압존법 at work.
- Brazilian Portuguese federal official writing: senhor/senhora only.
- **Plain target**: main point first; "you"; everyday words; active voice; must/need/can; sentences averaging 15–20 words, split above 25. Japanese やさしい日本語 rules only when asked for non-native readers. German Leichte Sprache drafts are labelled as needing testing with readers.

## Don't
- Add apologies, promises, praise or demands the original lacks.
- Change quotations; they keep their original register.
- Change fiction dialogue unless the user asks.

## Deliver
- Revise mode: replace proposals, one per paragraph.
- Review mode: the rewritten text in the reply.
- Reply in one sentence naming the new tone and anything you kept on purpose.
