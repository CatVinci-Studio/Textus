# Social post

## Scope
- The draft, notes or article in the selection, named passage or document, and the platform the user names. If no platform is named, ask once, or write one general version under 280 characters (measured with `chars`; don't name a platform the user didn't).

## Do
- Hook first: the point in the first line (LinkedIn folds at about 140–210 characters, 微博 at 140). One idea per post. Concrete over hype.
- Stay within the platform's limit and counting from the attached platform table (X counts CJK characters and emoji double and every URL as 23). Check each version with measure_text before proposing it (`x_weighted`, `bluesky`, `threads`, `instagram`, `mastodon`, `linkedin`, `wechat_title`, `wechat_digest`, `xhs_title`, `xhs_body`, `weibo`) and state the count in the reply ("X: 247/280").
- Hashtags by platform habit: Instagram at most 5, Bluesky at most 8, a few at the end elsewhere; none mid-sentence unless the platform's style uses them.
- Keep every figure, name, link and claim from the source.
- Disclosure: when the post promotes something for a business or is sponsored, add the disclosure the attached conventions require for the language and market, up front, not only in hashtags or behind "more".
- Chinese advertising law: treat absolute terms (最好, 第一, 顶级, 全网最…) as review flags and offer compliant wording; no health or disease claims; give a source for data. For mainland platforms, remind the user once to switch on the platform's AI-content declaration when the post is substantially AI-generated.

## Don't
- Invent prices, discounts, results, testimonials or endorsements.
- Present compliance notes as legal advice; they are review flags.
- Refuse because of an unverified limit (小红书, 微博); warn instead.

## Deliver
- Revise mode: replace the draft with the post when the document is a draft of it; when it is source material (a report, an article, product notes), keep it and insert the post after it under a short heading. Append one version per platform under a short heading when several are asked for.
- Write mode: insert the post after the source under a short heading, or append one version per platform; turning a draft into the post needs Revise mode.
- Review mode: the post in the reply.
- Reply with the count for each version ("X: 247/280") and any compliance flag in one line each.
