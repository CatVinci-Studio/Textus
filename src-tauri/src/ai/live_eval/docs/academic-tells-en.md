# Forecasting root-zone soil moisture

## Introduction

In recent years, soil-moisture forecasting has attracted increasing attention. Many methods exist \[3, 7, 9, 12, 15]. In this paper, we propose a novel framework that, to the best of our knowledge, is the first to delve into the intricate interplay between rainfall timing and root-zone drainage. We conduct extensive experiments on a wide range of sites, and the results prove that our method significantly outperforms all prior approaches, paving the way for a new paradigm in precision irrigation \[@okafor2022; @lindqvist2019].

## Results

Across the 38 stations, mean absolute error fell from 0.061 to 0.047 m³/m³ against the strongest baseline, DrainNet \[7]. The gain is larger in sandy soils (0.012 versus 0.009), which suggests that drainage timing matters more than rainfall volume at lead times beyond 72 h.
