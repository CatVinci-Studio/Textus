## What's new

Textus 1.3.7 makes a drag selection work wherever it starts, adds Format › Highlight, and keeps a `<ruby>` intact when a table cell is edited in place.

### New

- Highlights text from **Format › Highlight**, the right-click menu's Format, or Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+H, as in Typora; pressed again, it takes the highlight off. (#554)

### Fixed

- Dragging to select text works wherever the drag starts: on bold, highlighted or struck-through words, a heading, inline HTML or a wikilink, where it often selected nothing, and in the empty margin beside the text, which only placed the caret. The `**` or `#` of what you click shows once you let go of the mouse, not under the pointer mid-drag. (#554)
- Editing one cell of an HTML table in place no longer copies a `<ruby>` reading in another cell into its base text, and Escape no longer drops that reading. (#564)

Installers for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows and Linux (x64 and ARM64) are below; the app updates itself.
