## What's new

Textus 1.4.4 makes block dragging feel like moving an island, across documents open side by side too, adds heading folding and Plugin API 1.8, makes the first-run tour hands-on, and fixes the list handle, the caret's width, the Classic layout and many things on phones and tablets.

### New

- Shows ⋮⋮ beside the block you are writing in on a phone or tablet: tap it for the block's menu, or hold it to lift the block and move it.
- Folds a heading's section away in the document: click the arrow in the margin beside the heading, or press Cmd+Option+[ (Ctrl+Shift+[) in the section and Cmd+Option+] (Ctrl+Shift+]) to open it. The text itself never changes, and a search or the outline that lands inside a folded section opens it.
- Plugin API 1.8: plugins can add their own export formats, right-click menu items, blocks and ready-made snippets to the `/` menu, first steps on their page in Settings, marks in the margin beside a block, slips underlined and explained on the grammar check's card with a one-click fix, inline suggestions, inline syntax of their own (a spoiler, a reading over the words) shown without changing the text, notes in a document's margin read and left (with their own permission), and (with your OK, within a monthly limit) use the AI model you set up; front matter is read and written as full YAML, keeping your comments. The Git plugin uses the new margin marks for paragraphs changed since the last commit, and Tidy's Markdown rules now explain themselves on that card and fix the block they're in.

### Improved

- A block dragged by its handle lifts off the page as itself, with no frame; room the block's own size opens where it will land and the blocks around move aside, as a dragged tab does, and at a list's end there is one place to land, not one inside and another just outside. It can also be dragged into a document open beside it, and with Option (Ctrl on Windows and Linux) a copy goes instead.
- ⋮⋮ and a heading's fold arrow come together over any line of a block, after a short rest, and go as the mouse leaves; ＋ is gone from the margin (the `/` menu adds blocks). Settings › Editor › Block handles still turns ⋮⋮ off.
- A dragged block carries its ⋮⋮ along under the pointer, a code block or a table at its own size, and the only item of a list can be put back in its own place, between the headings around it.
- The strip that adds a row runs under the table only, not across the page.
- Blocks a plugin writes again in another order (Tasks' finished tasks moving last) slide to their new places instead of jumping.
- Appearance has a Window Layout section of its own (layout, activity bar, title bar row, menu bar), with a note on bringing back the look Textus had before 1.4.
- Lets appearance plugins add bundled grain to the window frame and keeps frame-only palettes from recoloring the document toolbar and other surfaces.
- Picks a custom endpoint's model from the list the endpoint serves, in Settings and in the chat's model menu, instead of typing its name. (#702)
- Runs the page down to the bottom of a phone's screen, under the home indicator or the gesture bar, instead of stopping above it.
- Lets plugins read and write the files of an Android folder opened without access to all files, as they do in any other folder. Moving a file to the Trash is left to the Files app there.
- Keeps fewer documents loaded out of sight on phones and tablets with less memory, and lets them go when the system runs short, as on a computer.
- Glides instead of jumping when the page follows the caret past the window's edge, when Find goes to a match (now centred), and in typewriter mode. A scroll or a click stops it, and Reduce Motion turns it off.
- A row or column added to a table opens up, the cells beside it sliding over; a row or column dragged in a table glides into the place it is let go.
- Dragging selected text, a file in the tree or a block near the top or bottom scrolls on at one pace; a rail icon and a plugin list's row are carried by a lifted copy that settles where they land.

### Fixed

- Typing stays quick in a document with many formulas: formulas, diagrams, HTML blocks and embedded notes are drawn only within a few screens of what you are reading and let go further away, also in a long document, where they used to stay drawn once seen. In a test with 300 formulas, a keystroke went from about 400 ms to under 30 ms. (#710)
- Keeps a list item's handle on that item as the mouse crosses its bullet or number to reach it, instead of jumping to the whole list, which left the item impossible to drag.
- Fails the custom endpoint's connection test when the endpoint doesn't serve the chosen model, instead of passing and then failing in the chat. (#700, #701)
- HTML and PDF exports carry the formulas' own fonts, so formulas keep their faces and spacing where KaTeX's fonts aren't installed. (#692)
- In a Chinese, Japanese or Korean interface on Windows, a letter with a combining mark (the bar of H̄ for a mean) shows the mark over the letter, not beside it. (#704)
- Switching the layout (or the interface size) in Settings lays the window out again at once, instead of keeping the islands' gaps around the documents until the sidebar was closed and opened again.
- Typing in Settings' search no longer stalls on the first letter or now and then after it: the pages it reads are read in the background once the box is focused, and not again with each letter.
- No light-blue frame stays round a block after it is dropped or picked whole; a picked block shows the theme's faint wash on every platform.
- A tab that comes to the front after a click (closing the tab beside it) no longer shows the keyboard's blue focus ring.
- Tabs in the Classic layout stand as tall as in the Islands layout, instead of filling the whole row.
- Gives the AI the folder open in the window's sidebar whenever no other folder is chosen for it, instead of a folder another window opened or none after the choice was cleared.
- Opens a grammar suggestion's card as soon as the caret moves into its underline, not one move later.
- Reopens a database, board or other file a plugin shows after the app restarts on a phone, in its place among the tabs, instead of leaving it out.
- Keeps the paragraph you were reading at the top when a tablet turns or a window or island changes width, instead of the same distance from the top.
- Opens a database (`.base`) picked with Open… on Android, which showed that the plugin couldn't show its page.
- The tour lights each activity bar button on its own and opens its panel as it goes, and its writing step opens a practice document ready to type in, instead of a dark window when the tour began from the Start page, and lists a title, a list, a numbered list and a to-do to try, ticking each off as it is typed.
- The window opens in its layout from the first frame, without a dark line by the activity bar for a moment, and plugins' icons in the activity bar and the window bar no longer show as grey squares while they load.
- Cmd+Enter (Ctrl+Enter) in a table continues below the table, as the shortcut list says.
- Shows the plugins' icon for a plugin's sidebar tab that has none of its own, instead of a filled square.
- Draws the caret at the same thin width at any document zoom, on whole screen pixels, instead of growing thicker as the document is zoomed in and blurring at some places in the line; it follows a zoom or a narrower window at once.
- Keeps Textus's own caret while Chinese, Japanese or Korean is being typed with an input method, instead of switching to the system's for the length of each word.
- With the document zoomed in or out, table rows and blocks making room or settling slide from where they were, instead of overshooting by the zoom.
- Esc while widening an image by its grip puts it back as it was.
