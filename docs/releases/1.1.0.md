## What's new

Textus 1.1.0 brings a new first-run tour, autosave, themes you can install from inside the app, a choice of where pasted images are saved or uploaded to, Obsidian image embeds, more HTML in your documents, and a beta update channel.

### New

- Starts new users with a rebuilt tour: connect your AI first, then the main features one step at a time, each with a short animation to watch; skip the AI and its lessons show you instead. (#90)
- Shows collapsible `<details>` blocks, HTML tables, a few inline styles (colour, size, weight, alignment) and inline `<svg>` drawings as they look, while you write, in split view and in HTML and PDF exports; scripts in a document never run. (#128)
- Lets you choose **Settings › General › About Textus › Update channel › Beta** to get release candidates before everyone else; Stable stays the default. (#127)
- Saves documents back to their file as you write when **Settings › General › Save Automatically** is on (off by default); a file changed on disk by another app is never overwritten. (#133)
- Browses and installs themes from the theme gallery in **Settings › Editor & Appearance › Browse themes**, and offers updates when a theme changes. (#89)
- Shows Obsidian image embeds such as `![[image.png]]`, found the way Obsidian finds them in your vault, and saves them back exactly as written. (#135)
- Lets you type closing `*`, `**`, `~~` and `==` yourself: turn off **Settings › Editor & Appearance › Close formatting marks automatically**. (#136)
- Opens the video tutorials from **Help › Video Tutorials**. (#119)
- Lets you choose where pasted and dropped images are saved in **Settings › Editor & Appearance › Images**: an `assets` folder, next to the document, a folder named after the document, or a folder of your own, each shown with the link it writes. (#147)
- Uploads pasted images through PicGo or PicList (to any service set up there), SM.MS or Imgur, as well as to your own upload endpoint. (#164)

### Improved

- Remembers whether the sidebar was open, so it comes back the way you left it. (#152)
- Writes AI replies out smoothly, a little each frame, instead of in sudden chunks; stopping shows everything received at once. (#168)
- Moves **Import from Levis** into **Settings › General › About Textus**. (#162)
- Updates KaTeX, which draws formulas. (#114)
- Keeps up to 500 past conversations in the sidebar's Chats tab instead of 60, and reopens each one with its own document; older ones are moved to an archive folder rather than deleted. (#PR)
- Greys out the margin views of **Show revisions** when the sidebar or the AI panel leaves no room for a margin, and says why; your choice is kept and comes back on its own once there is room, without the page jumping. (#165)

### Fixed

- Turns `**bold**`, `*italic*` and the other marks into formatting when the `*` comes from a Chinese input method. (#134)
- Shows images whose relative path contains escapes such as `%20`. (#135)
- Ticks a task checkbox in Typewriter Mode without the page jumping. (#136)
- Keeps what you type after `***` inside the bold-italic text instead of dropping it. (#148)
- Follows links with `Ctrl` + click (`⌘` + click on a Mac): another Markdown file opens in a new tab, a `#heading` scrolls to it, and web links open in your browser instead of a broken page. (#152)
- Keeps the top and bottom page margins clear in exported PDFs when a code block, table, quote or list runs across pages. (#151)
- Keeps the Settings window the same width when you switch sections. (#163)
- Keeps an AI note's popover under the Settings window instead of drawing it on top. (#167)
- Keeps an AI revision's struck original and new text on screen when you point at them; pointing now outlines that pair and its card in the margin. (#176)
- Hides the "Start writing in Markdown…" hint in an empty document while Textus AI writes into it, instead of showing it over the new text. (#166)

### Platform notes

- **macOS:** lets you drag the window while the first-launch language card is open (#149), and now requires macOS 11 or later.
- **Windows:** Source Code Mode and code use a monospaced font again instead of falling back to SimSun. (#132)
- **Windows:** runs natively on ARM64 PCs such as Snapdragon laptops, with its own installer, `_arm64-setup.exe`. (#175)
- **Linux:** runs natively on ARM64, as an AppImage, `.deb` or `.rpm`; the Arch package stays x86_64. (#175)

Installers for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows and Linux (x64 and ARM64) are below; the app updates itself.
