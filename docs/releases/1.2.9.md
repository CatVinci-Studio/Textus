## What's new

Textus 1.2.9 makes Undo and Redo on macOS take back every kind of edit.

### Fixed

- On macOS, Cmd+Z, Cmd+Shift+Z and Edit › Undo / Redo take back pastes, cuts, formatting, headings and moved paragraphs, not just some typing. In a text field, a plugin's panel or a web page tab they undo there, and never the document behind.
- Each paste, cut or drop is an undo step of its own: two quick pastes, or a paste and the typing right after it, are no longer taken back together.
- Cmd+B and Cmd+I format text across several paragraphs or after Select All, and press again to take it off.
- With all plugins turned off, the plugin list is dimmed as a whole instead of showing a red note on every card.

Installers for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows and Linux (x64 and ARM64) are below; the app updates itself.
