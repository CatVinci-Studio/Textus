## What's new

Textus 1.3.0-beta.6 splits the window into as many panes as you like, brings each window back with its tabs and panes when you reopen Textus, and brings 1.2.7's fixes.

### New

- More than two documents at once: Split Right and Split Down split the pane in use again, side by side or one above the other, as many times as you like. A tab dropped on a pane's edge opens in a new pane on that side, each line between panes resizes the two beside it, and the whole layout comes back when you reopen the window. A tab dragged to the outer edge opens beside all the panes: at the right edge of two panes one above the other, it fills the right side top to bottom. (#432)

### Improved

- Help › Report a Problem adds your settings to the report, without anything private: switches, numbers and choices, never folders, addresses, servers, your custom CSS or your own words. You see it all before sending.

### Fixed

- Reopening Textus brings each window back with its own tabs, and a split with its panes, instead of opening every document of the last session in a window of its own.
- A plugin's button and the menu arrow beside it join into one button again, instead of two shapes side by side.
- In the picture and diagram viewer, pinching or Ctrl-scrolling zooms the picture, never the document behind it, and a darker backdrop sets the viewer apart from the page.
- A copied picture pastes into WeChat and other chat apps as the picture, not as its Markdown text.
- In source mode and in code, letters such as `fi` are no longer drawn joined as one glyph, so the cursor can stop between them. (#438)

Installers for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows and Linux (x64 and ARM64) are below; the app updates itself.
