## What's new

Textus 1.3.0-beta.6 splits the window into as many panes as you like, brings each window back with its tabs and panes, adds Back and Forward, moving paragraphs with Alt+↑/↓, turning pictures and pinyin search, lets you send a problem report from inside Textus, tidies Settings, and brings 1.2.7's and 1.2.8's fixes.

### New

- More than two documents at once: Split Right and Split Down split the pane in use again, side by side or one above the other, as many times as you like. A tab dropped on a pane's edge opens in a new pane on that side, each line between panes resizes the two beside it, and the whole layout comes back when you reopen the window. A tab dragged to the outer edge opens beside all the panes: at the right edge of two panes one above the other, it fills the right side top to bottom. (#432)
- Right-click a picture to turn it a quarter left or right, or to view it large. The turn is saved in the picture's title, so the Markdown stays standard. Thanks to @jzzzzh. (#458)
- Alt+↑ and Alt+↓ (Option on a Mac) move the paragraph or list item you're in, or every block you've selected, above or below its neighbour.
- Back and Forward: after following a link, clicking a heading in the outline or switching tabs, Ctrl+- (Alt+← on Windows and Linux) returns to where you were, and Ctrl+Shift+- (Alt+→) goes on again. The mouse's back and forward buttons do the same.
- File search and the command palette find Chinese names typed in pinyin, in full or by initials: `beisong`, `bs` or `beis` finds 背诵. Accents can be left out too: `cafe` finds café.

### Improved

- Report a Problem is in Settings › General's Help group too, and adds your settings to the report without anything private: switches, numbers and choices, never folders, addresses, servers, your custom CSS or your own words. It asks what kind of report it is, what part of Textus it is about and, for a bug, how bad it is, and takes an email or WeChat if you're happy to be asked more. **Send** files it for you as an issue on GitHub, with no GitHub account needed, and says its number; **Submit on GitHub** opens GitHub's form instead. A contact you leave goes to the team only, never into the public issue.
- Source mode, Source and Preview, zen mode and typewriter mode are one setting for all of Textus: switch one in any window - from the View menu, a shortcut or Settings - and every tab of every window switches with it, the documents you open next come up in it, and Textus opens in it again next time.
- Settings is tidier. General › Help has five rows - Welcome and Tutorial (the tour again), Guides, Report a Problem, Join the Community and About Textus, where the updates are. Editor & Appearance and Textus AI keep what you change often up front and fold the rest under Advanced; pictures Textus AI makes have a group of their own.
- One name for each thing, in the menus, Settings and Shortcuts alike: Split View is now **Source and Preview**, so it isn't mistaken for Split Right and Split Down, and the image upload setting says **Image host**.
- On a Mac, **Textus › Check for Updates…** opens About and checks at once.
- The command palette changes the appearance (light, dark or the system's), the editor theme, and turns Textus AI on or off.
- Themes from Get more can come with a dark form of their own, which they switch to along with your appearance, light or dark.

### Fixed

- Reopening Textus brings each window back with its own tabs, and a split with its panes, instead of opening every document of the last session in a window of its own.
- A plugin's button and the menu arrow beside it join into one button again, instead of two shapes side by side.
- In the picture and diagram viewer, pinching or Ctrl-scrolling zooms the picture, never the document behind it, and a darker backdrop sets the viewer apart from the page.
- A copied picture pastes into WeChat and other chat apps as the picture, not as its Markdown text.
- In source mode and in code, letters such as `fi` are no longer drawn joined as one glyph, so the cursor can stop between them. (#438)
- A plugin whose code holds `<!--` and `<script>` - a bundled Markdown parser has both - runs again instead of stopping before its commands are added.
- With an input method such as Microsoft Pinyin, a `*` typed in front of text that already ends in `*` makes it italic, as it does without one.
- On Windows, a numbered list that runs onto the next page of an exported PDF goes on counting there instead of starting again.
