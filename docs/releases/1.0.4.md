## What's new

Textus 1.0.4 brings a resizable sidebar and page numbers in PDFs, fixes PDF export on Windows and Linux, and makes the installers smaller.

### New

- Lets you drag the sidebar's edge to make it wider or narrower, and remembers the width. (#77)
- Adds page numbers to exported PDFs when **Settings › Editor & Appearance › Markdown › Page numbers in PDF** is on (off by default). (#68)
- Adds **Settings › General › About Textus**, with the version, the update check and links to GitHub and the community.
- Opens the community page in your language from **Help › Join the Community**, with Discord and the Chinese QQ and WeChat groups.

### Improved

- Installs from smaller downloads: the Linux app is less than half its old size. (#115)
- Updates the editor's dependencies, including two with security advisories.

### Fixed

- Prints exported PDFs on white with proper margins on Windows and Linux, and no longer drops lines at the bottom of a page. (#68)
- Exports a PDF on Windows with `Ctrl + P` instead of printing the window. (#68)
- Keeps text sharp while you edit in Source Code Mode on Linux. (#61)

### Platform notes

- **Windows:** opens files dropped on the window, and **File › Open** lets you pick any file. (#78)

Installers for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows and Linux are below; the app updates itself.
