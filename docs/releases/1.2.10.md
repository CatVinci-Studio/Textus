## What's new

Textus 1.2.10 keeps links and bold words that Textus AI writes into a sentence, and makes Replace All one undo.

### Fixed

- Text Textus AI writes into the middle of a sentence - or an item put in from the clipboard history - keeps a wiki link or bold words it starts with, instead of turning them into plain text.
- One undo takes back a whole Replace All, and only it, even right after another edit.

Installers for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows and Linux (x64 and ARM64) are below; the app updates itself.
