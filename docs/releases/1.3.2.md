## What's new

Textus 1.3.2 lets you set the document's text size and line spacing yourself, brings nine new looks to the theme gallery, and draws every plugin with Textus's own controls, menus and colours.

### New

- **Settings › Editor & Appearance › Document text size** and **Line spacing**: the document's text from 14 to 24 px and its lines compact, standard or relaxed, kept from the moment Textus opens. A theme can bring its own; yours wins over it.
- New looks in the theme gallery: One Dark, Rosé Pine, Tokyo Night and Solarized, and five that set their own type and spacing - Cards, Compact, Contrast, Reading Room and Outline - each in light and dark. Our own near-duplicates leave the gallery; themes you've installed stay.

### Improved

- Plugin pages are drawn with Textus's own controls - buttons, fields, segmented controls, tags, tables, Settings' groups - from the same rules as the app, and their menus are Textus's own menus, so a plugin looks and behaves like the rest of the window and follows every theme.
- Tags, board lists and charts in plugins take their colours from the theme: a theme with a palette of its own colours them too.
- A plugin's block that reads as part of the page, such as a database, sits in the document without a box around it.
- Textus has one built-in look, the default. Paper, Slate, Forest and Parchment are withdrawn; a document set to one shows the default, and the theme gallery has looks in their spirit.

### Fixed

- A block a plugin draws in a document - a database, a chart, a link card - shrinks back when what it shows gets shorter, such as a table after a filter, instead of keeping its tallest height or growing to its limit.
- Vim keys: **zz**, **zt** and **zb** bring the caret's line to the middle, top or bottom of the window (with Vim Keys 0.2.2), and normal mode's block cursor is a square cell instead of a rounded pill.

Installers for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows and Linux (x64 and ARM64) are below; the app updates itself.
