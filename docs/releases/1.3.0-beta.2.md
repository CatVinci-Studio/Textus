## What's new

Textus 1.3.0-beta.2 puts plugins, themes and skills in one market, opens two documents side by side, previews links in a card, and lets Textus AI work on a long document a chapter at a time and remember better.

### New

- One market for plugins, themes and skills: Settings › Extensions › Get more lists all three, with one search and a tab for each kind, and installs a skill in a click. How to list your own is linked at the bottom.
- Two documents at once, with the Split Panes plugin (Settings › Extensions › Get more): Split Right (Ctrl+Alt+S, ⌘⌥S on a Mac) shows the document in front beside another tab, and Split Down puts one above the other. Drag the line between them to share the space, and double-click it to halve it again. A click in a pane brings its document to the front, and a tab you choose from the tab bar opens in that pane. Plugins can split the window too, through `textus.window.splitEditor`.
- Quick looks at what a link points to, with the Quick Preview plugin: rest the pointer on a link to another document - a Markdown link or a [[wikilink]] - and its opening, or the section the link names, shows in a card beside it. Pin keeps the card open, to move where you like while you write. With the Zotero plugin, resting on a citation such as [@darwin1859] shows what it cites. Plugins can fill such cards through `textus.editor.registerHoverProvider`.
- Search your conversations: the AI panel's history has a search field that finds the conversations where words were said, by you or by Textus AI, and shows the passage. Textus AI looks there too when you refer to an earlier conversation - "the name we picked last week".
- A task worth doing again becomes a skill: when what you asked for was a procedure of your own - the sections of your weekly report, how your newsletters are translated - Textus AI offers to keep it as a skill under its reply. Save it for every document or for this project, and it is in the `/` menu from then on. Nothing is saved unless you choose to.
- Redraws a proposed picture from your own description: point at the picture and choose Redraw…, change what it shows, and that picture alone is drawn again - the rest of the proposal stays as it is.

### Improved

- Pictures in one piece keep the same people and look: Textus AI describes someone who comes back in the same words each time, and where the image model takes a picture to work from (ChatGPT, Seedream, Gemini), a new picture of them is drawn from an earlier one that is already made.
- Web pages in a tab (the Web View plugin) open every site on macOS and Windows: the page is the system's own web view laid over the tab, with its own cookies, so sites that refused to show in a frame - GitHub, Google and many more - now work, and side by side with a document through Split Panes. A link that leaves the web opens in your browser. On Linux the page stays a frame for now.
- Long documents change chapter by chapter at once: asked to translate or rewrite the whole of a long document in chapters, Textus AI hands each chapter to a helper of its own, with a shared list of how the recurring names and terms are written, and the chapters' edits come in together. A 42,000-character report in eleven chapters was translated into English in under four minutes, where three of its chapters alone took three minutes before.
- Textus AI's memory keeps itself tidy: now and then - after six new memories, or a week - it merges memories that say the same thing and drops a rule a newer one overturned, keeping exceptions to a rule. What it drops is in the Memory tab's Recently forgotten, to bring back.

### Fixed

- The official plugins speak all ten of Textus's languages - Traditional Chinese no longer shows Simplified, nor the other languages English - and a plugin of your own can too, with `textus.l10n.t` and `api.t` reading its manifest's `l10n`.
- In Graph View, notes in a small folder keep apart instead of drawing their names over one another.

Installers for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows and Linux (x64 and ARM64) are below; the app updates itself.
