## What's new

Textus 1.4.2 brings Textus to Android as an APK, makes formatting toggles merge what a selection already has, folds a split away in a narrow window, adds a sponsor link and Copy Table, and fixes many things found in an audit.

### New

- Plugins: `textus.export.mathCss()` gives KaTeX's stylesheet with its fonts inside, so a plugin page or a saved file shows formulas offline (plugin API 1.6).
- Windows: a portable zip beside the installer. Unzip it anywhere and run Textus.exe; its settings and data stay in the TextusData folder next to it. It tells you about new versions; to update, unzip the new one over the folder.
- Android: Textus as an APK for phones and tablets, one download for both. Open files and folders through the system's picker, save them back, search a folder, and pick up where you left off.
- iPhone: the same Textus as on the desktop, in one column. The tab row has the menu, the sidebar button, your tabs, + and Textus AI; the AI opens as a tab beside your documents.
- iPhone: the sidebar and its icons slide in from the left and push the page aside; tap the page, swipe or open a file to push it back.
- iPhone: the page is one rounded sheet on the frame; while you type, the format bar rides above the keyboard, with undo, a table's or a picture's own buttons where the cursor is, and a key to put the keyboard away.
- iPhone and iPad: Open Folder. Pick a folder in Files and work in it as on the desktop.
- Plugins can change Textus's colours: a palette over whichever theme is on, light and dark apart, and the frame round your pages may be a gradient across the whole window. Text stays readable, your own CSS still wins, and turning the plugin off puts the theme back (plugin API 1.6, permission "appearance").
- A new built-in theme, Aurora: a soft gradient from sky blue into pink across the frame round your pages, deep sea into plum in dark mode, with the page itself left as it is.
- Plugins can do more of what you'd expect from VS Code and Obsidian extensions: handle what you paste, put a small badge on files in the sidebar, make links in your text clickable, add their own entries to the Outline, offer searchable pick lists, follow where you are reading, show a status in the window bar from the moment Textus opens, and hear of files you make or move to the Trash (plugin API 1.6).
- Help › Sponsor Textus, and a small link under the version in Settings › About, open the sponsor page (not on iOS).
- Tables: Copy Table in the right-click menu copies the whole table as Markdown.

### Improved

- A plugin's page puts Uninstall beside its version, next to Source and Edit with AI, instead of at the bottom of the page.
- Pinching to zoom a long document stays smooth: the page grows under your fingers and is laid out at the new size once you let go.
- iPad and Android tablets use the desktop layout: the icon rail, the sidebar, side-by-side documents and dragging tabs. A narrow window (Slide Over, a small split) switches to the phone layout.
- With no AI signed in, Textus AI's panel says so and offers a Sign in button that opens Settings, instead of a message box that sends nowhere.
- A new, shorter welcome tour. It lights one part of the window at a time and dims the rest: the sidebar and its buttons, Settings, the editor, then Textus AI. Steps that ask you to do something move on once you do it. Signing in to an AI is optional; without one the tour skips the AI.
- On phones and tablets, Settings › About shows the app is a Beta while it is in public testing.
- Textus AI's Memory page is simpler: its folder sits on the top row, a × (or Esc) goes back to the conversation, each memory shows in a line or two and opens for its details and actions, the writing style, instructions and library are plain rows, and with nothing kept yet they are one row of buttons.
- Android: a folder opened from the device's storage opens by its plain path once Textus is allowed to access all files (asked the first time), so plugins, search and file watching work on it as on the desktop. Without that permission the folder opens as before. Settings › Extensions now lists plugins, themes and skills on Android too.
- Android: on a device without the all-files permission screen, or for a memory card's folder on Android 10 and earlier, the folder opens as before rather than failing or opening read-only. A whole storage volume is never opened as a plain path.
- Linux: the window's buttons are small and round, close together as in GNOME's own apps; the menu button matches the sidebar's in size and tone.

### Fixed

- In an imported RTF document, a list item whose marker only looks like a Roman numeral, such as `iiii.`, `vx.` or `ic.`, comes in as a bullet instead of being numbered 4, 5 or 99. (#671)
- Messages waiting to be sent pause again when a reply fails the same way twice in a row, instead of going out one after another.
- In the sidebar's Plugins list, a plugin that needs a newer Textus says which version on its button, instead of showing no button.
- The sidebar's name no longer runs under the window bar's buttons (the sidebar button, the menu) when the activity rail is turned off or in full screen.
- The Find and Replace bar stays at the top of the document as you scroll, in the rich editor as in Source mode.
- Linux: menus, settings and dialogs no longer flash as they open; Textus now uses WebKitGTK's older renderer on every Linux system, not only with NVIDIA's driver (set `WEBKIT_DISABLE_DMABUF_RENDERER=0` to go back).
- Plugin pages (a database, a board) show their scroll bars only while they scroll, like the rest of Textus; they no longer stay up after a scroll.
- Highlighted text is readable in dark mode again: it kept the browser's black instead of the text colour.
- Ghost-text completion and the grammar check no longer fill the log with errors when no AI provider is signed in; after a failed request they wait half a minute before asking again.
- Pictures attached to a message now reach an outside agent (Claude Code, Codex and others) when the agent can read images; before, only the text was sent.
- Ticking a task with Tasks' "Finished tasks last" on no longer jumps the page to the top: a plugin rewriting a block leaves the view and the caret where they were.
- In a narrow window with a split, the tab row beside the window's buttons keeps room for its open tab: the bare strip kept for dragging the window gives way first, and the island under those buttons is the last to shrink.
- Bold, italic, strikethrough, highlight and inline code on a selection that already holds some formatting now format all of it: the same formatting inside merges into one span (inline code gives up any formatting, which code can't hold), and a span the selection cuts is taken in whole, bold italic included. Before, the shortcut did nothing. Pressed again on a selection of all of a span's text, they take it off, across several lines too.
- Backspace at the start of a numbered item takes its number away: the item becomes text between two lists, and the items after it start again from 1. Before, it joined the item above and the numbers ran on.
- Backspace at the start of a task takes its checkbox away first, leaving a plain list item, instead of dropping the box along with the line.
- Phone: after the sidebar was opened and closed, the tab row could show no tabs at all, and a newly opened tab sat half under the + button. The open tab now always shows whole.
- When tabs overflow, the + button stays clear of the tab list button at every interface size.
- Phone: plugins' floating views (the Desk Pet) step aside while the keyboard is up, instead of covering the format strip and the text being typed.
- An outside agent that can't read images now says so when a message carries one, instead of answering without it.
- Esc ends the tour only when nothing else takes it: closing completion, the find bar or a menu, or typing, no longer ends it. Back in the tour no longer bounces on a step that is already done, the tour no longer skips a step when a panel opens with two quick updates, and it dims the window again after a step that left it undimmed.
- A plugin rewriting a block (Tasks sorting a list) keeps your selected text selected.
- iPad and Android tablets: an island split under another no longer leaves a status bar's height of empty space above its tabs.
- The window's top-right island keeps room for the bar's buttons when a status appears there, and when those buttons need more than an island's least width.
- Phone: a sideways swipe that scrolls a long name, a table or a text field no longer opens or closes the sidebar.
- A window too narrow for its split folds one island into the one beside it, after the sidebar and the AI's island have made room, and puts it back, with the document you were on still in use, once there is room again.
- A plugin's badge on a file shows on Windows, in subfolders too, and in a folder opened through a link on macOS; a plugin can take a badge off a file that was deleted or renamed.
- Android: the phone menu no longer lists Documents, which only iOS has; picking up a tab gives a light haptic as on iOS; Word files open from plain paths.
- Typing Chinese with an input method into a new heading no longer leaves the text in front of its "##".

Installers for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows and Linux (x64 and ARM64), and the Android APK, are below; the desktop app updates itself.
