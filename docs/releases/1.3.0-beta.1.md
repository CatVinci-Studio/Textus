## What's new

Textus 1.3.0-beta.1 is the first beta of 1.3: plugins you install from inside Textus, your own notes and tracked changes in the margin beside Textus AI's, the model switched from the chat, unsaved work kept across a restart, and themes in one place. It has everything in 1.2.1 too. A beta may still have rough edges - tell us on GitHub or Discord.

### New

- Plugins: Settings › Extensions › Plugins lists the plugins you installed, each opening its own page, and Get plugins offers the rest on a page of their own, with a search by name, what a plugin does or who made it.
- Your own notes: select some words, right-click › Add Note, and your note goes into the margin beside Textus AI's, marked Me. Edit or delete it on its card. Notes are kept beside Textus's own data, not in your Markdown, and come back on their words when the document opens again; a file renamed or moved outside Textus loses them.
- Track My Changes (Edit menu): with it on, your edits to the document show as changes - what you deleted struck through where it was, what you added marked - each with a card in the margin, marked Me, to accept or reject; right-click to accept or reject them all. Your text is saved as you write it, and the original it is compared with is kept beside Textus's own data, so tracking carries on when the document opens again.
- Switches the chat's model from the message box: the button beside the other controls names the model your next message goes to, and its list holds the providers you have set up in **Settings › Textus AI** - signed in, a key saved, or none needed - with their models. Choosing one is the same as choosing it in Settings.
- Keeps unsaved changes when you close a window, without asking, once you turn on **Settings › Privacy › Keep unsaved changes when closing**: the documents open again with their changes the next time Textus starts, as after a crash, and the files themselves stay as they were until you save. Off by default; it needs Draft Recovery on.

### Improved

- Get plugins shows the list from last time at once while it fetches a newer one, and keeps showing it when you're offline.
- Themes have one place: Settings › Extensions › Themes holds adding a theme from a CSS file, your own CSS and the theme gallery, while Editor & Appearance keeps picking a theme, with Get more themes leading there. The settings search finds the Themes rows too.

### Platform notes

- Windows and Linux: Settings › Editor & Appearance › Show the menu bar puts File, Edit, View and the rest along the title bar instead of behind ☰ - off by default. With it on, the sidebar button makes room for them (the sidebar is in View and has its shortcut), and a window too narrow for the titles gets ☰ back.

Installers for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows and Linux (x64 and ARM64) are below; the app updates itself.
