## What's new

Textus 1.4.0 makes Textus an Integrated Writing Environment: documents, Textus AI, Settings and plugin pages side by side in one window, each in an island of its own, with Textus AI in tabs of its own and a lighter, faster start.

### New

- A new window: documents, Textus AI, Settings and plugin pages sit side by side in islands under one title bar that holds their tabs. Drag a tab to an island's edge to split it, or onto another island to join it; hold `Alt` to move a whole island.
- Textus AI opens in tabs of its own, each named Textus AI with its conversation's title on hover: **+** beside History starts a new conversation in a tab beside the one you have, a conversation reopened from History or carried on from Quick Ask gets a tab of its own, and the conversations follow the window's folder.
- Choose how Textus AI changes files in **Settings › Textus AI**: as revisions to review (the default) or written straight in. As revisions, a change to a file that isn't open waits in that file and shows as revisions when you open it; the file tree and its tab say how many wait there, and a change that meets your own edits is listed instead of lost. A file Textus AI makes waits for you to keep it or discard it (to the Trash). Written straight in, the marks of what it changed stay after a restart.
- Claude Code and Codex can answer in Textus AI as external agents, each through its own plugin.
- Hold `Ctrl/⌘` for a moment in a window with two or more islands and each shows a number; `Ctrl/⌘ + 1`–`6` then goes to that island.
- Zoom all documents together (**Settings › Editing**): one zoom sizes every document at once.
- Source mode has the same find and replace bar as the rendered view, with `Ctrl/⌘ + F`. (#642)
- A first-line indent for body text (**Settings › Editing › Text**), and Appearance and Editing as two Settings pages. (#638)
- Plugins can use the network for the hosts they declare, show pictures from those hosts, go full screen (Slides) and hear when a picture you paste or drop is saved in the folder.
- Each window keeps its own settings for its folder and its AI, and a reload brings back its plugin tabs and unsaved drafts.

### Improved

- Textus starts faster: the first load is about a third lighter, and the editor gets ready while the window is idle.
- Only the tab in front of each island keeps an editor: a tab you come back to opens at its text with its undo history, caret and reading place, and many open tabs no longer each hold a whole editor.
- The islands, their tabs and the sidebar move as one: opening the AI or a split grows the new island in from its edge while the others make room.
- Every panel fits its width: rows, headers, buttons and the composer step down or wrap instead of being cut off, in every language. The interface's size follows Interface Text Size only, not the window. (#647)
- One look across the app: solid surfaces, the same rows, badges, empty states and menus in Settings, the sidebar and plugin pages.
- A conversation with Textus AI belongs to the window, not to one document's editor: it stays as it is when you close its document or bring another to the front.
- Revisions and review notes share one bar at the bottom of the document; its arrows step through both in the document's order.
- The format bar is on by default and sits at the top of the document you are working in.
- A diagram takes the code block's look from your theme. (#643)
- Dragging a table column's edge changes only that column, also in a wide table, and the add-row button sits clear of the table's scrollbar. (#645)
- Moving a file or folder to another disk copies it, keeps its links and conversations, and then moves the original to the Trash.
- Tabs in a crowded row share it evenly: long names give way first.
- New Window is in the File menu, under New Tab (`Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + N`).

### Fixed

- Track My Changes' **Reject All** puts back only the text you changed: notes and Textus AI's undecided edits elsewhere in the document stay.
- A maths preview stays under its block in the document instead of moving to the window's corner. (#641)
- Text asked into Textus AI ("Ask about it", a plugin's "Edit with AI") goes to the AI tab on screen.
- The "Writing a continuation…" and "Checking grammar…" notes go away as soon as the result is in, and a grammar underline is never placed beside its words after an edit above it.
- Outline rows and their copied links keep underscores inside words, code and maths (`get_user_name`).
- A sidebar Search hit opens at its own line, not the block after it.
- **Stop** keeps a picture Textus AI already made.
- Signing in again after leaving a sign-in unfinished works at once.
- Updating from **Settings** or **Help › Check for Updates** restarts Textus on Linux as the update button does.
- A plugin update whose new permission you refuse is turned off instead of staying on half-working.
- A pasted, dropped or chosen `.avif` picture is saved; a nested image folder (`img/shots`) is kept after a restart.
- A title ending in `#` (`Learning C#`) keeps it in a Pandoc export with numbered headings.
- Opening the AI in a mid-width window puts it beside the document instead of over it.
- The plugin store compares test versions by number, so Claude Code and Codex install again; a plugin list shows a page's own icon.
- A single tab no longer shows its name cut short after a font loads, and an open tab's corner no longer turns square after swapping islands.

### Platform notes

- **Windows:** dragging a file or folder in the sidebar keeps its tabs, conversations and links pointing at the right place.

Installers for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows and Linux (x64 and ARM64) are below; the app updates itself.
