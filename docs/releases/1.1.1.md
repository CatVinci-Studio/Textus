## What's new

### Improved

- Darkens the revision you point at, or step to in the review bar, the way a review note does - a darker green for the new text and a darker red for the original, in the text and in its margin card - instead of outlining it. (#207)

Installers for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows and Linux (x64 and ARM64) are below; the app updates itself.
