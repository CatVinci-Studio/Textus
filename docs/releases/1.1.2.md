## What's new

Textus 1.1.2 fixes how the Linux packages describe Textus.

### Platform notes

- **Linux:** Describes Textus in the `.deb` and `.rpm` packages, so `apt show textus` and `dnf info textus` show what it is instead of "(none)", and gives the `.rpm` its MIT license instead of "Unspecified".

Installers for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows and Linux (x64 and ARM64) are below; the app updates itself.
