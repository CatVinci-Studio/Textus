### Platform notes

- iPad: Export as PDF, laid out as on a Mac, then the share sheet to save it to Files or send it; on a missing picture, Choose Image… picks one from Files; drop-down lists, the colour picker and a code block's language menu are the desktop's; the share sheet opens where you tapped; a table's row and column handles lift only once held, so a long press beside the first column gives the paste bar.
