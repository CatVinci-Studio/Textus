### Fixed

- Windows: windows open in the theme's own background, dark or light, with no white flash at launch, on maximize or when coming back. (#690)
- Linux: windows have GNOME's shadow and can be resized from just outside their edge, and open at the size they had. (#664)
- The welcome tour's card narrows beside the sidebar instead of covering it.

### Improved

- A split made into a narrow window first folds the side panels, then puts the sidebar away, so the documents keep their room; when even that isn't enough, the window says so instead of doing nothing.
- The built-in theme Aurora is now called Prism; if you used it, pick Prism again in Settings › Appearance.
