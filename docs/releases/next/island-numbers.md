### New

- Hold `Ctrl/⌘` for a moment in a window with two or more islands and each shows a number; `Ctrl/⌘ + 1`–`6` then goes to that island. Pressed at once, the keys make headings as before.

### Improved

- Tabs in a crowded row share it evenly: long names give way first, and a short name keeps its whole tab.
