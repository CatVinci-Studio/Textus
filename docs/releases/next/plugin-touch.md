### Fixed

- On a touchscreen, the small buttons and checkboxes in plugin pages are easier to tap: each takes a fingertip-sized tap around it, and looks as before. On a phone, the round icon buttons in plugin pages are round again instead of oval.
- Holding a finger on a row in a plugin's sidebar list (Tags, Bookmarks, Clipboard History, Backlinks) shows its menu, as a right-click does.
- Lists in plugin pages that are reordered by dragging, such as a board's lists, can now be dragged by touch: hold for half a second, then drag. The page doesn't scroll under your finger while you drag, and scrolls by itself when you drag near its edge.
- On Android, pinching with two fingers zooms PDFs, pictures, Word documents and the graph, as it does on other devices.
