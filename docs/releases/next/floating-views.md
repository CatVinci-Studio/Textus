### New

- Plugins can show a floating view: a small transparent page that floats over your desktop, above every window, or inside Textus's window - a desk pet, a timer. It starts in the window; drag it anywhere, resize it from its top corner, and right-click it to put it on the desktop, fold it to a dot or close it - on the desktop its menu also opens Textus or Textus AI. It opens again where you left it. A plugin can also hear that you poked, petted or moved its view, that you typed or saved, and what Textus AI is doing and with which model - never a word of your documents or conversations. A plugin can add its own items to the view's right-click menu (`menus` `floating/context`), such as switching the desk pet's character. See `contributes.floatingViews`, `layout.openFloating`, `app.onDidActivity` and `ai.onDidChangeState` in the plugin reference.
