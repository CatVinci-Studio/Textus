### New

- Plugins can show a floating view: a small transparent page that floats over your desktop, above every window, or inside Textus's window - a desk pet, a timer. Drag it by the bar over its top, fold it to a dot or close it; it opens again where you left it. A plugin can also hear that you typed or saved, and what Textus AI is doing and with which model - never a word of your documents or conversations. See `contributes.floatingViews`, `layout.openFloating`, `app.onDidActivity` and `ai.onDidChangeState` in the plugin reference.
