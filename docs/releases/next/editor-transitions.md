### Fixed

- A diagram in a tab you open again shows its drawing at once, instead of its source for a moment, and a diagram drawn above where you are reading no longer moves the page.
- Diagrams take the new colours when you switch between two imported themes that set different diagram palettes.
- A link's preview card opens at its full size in its place, instead of opening small and then growing over the link.
- A code block's line numbers stay beside their lines while the window is resized.
- The first document with maths you open no longer shows an empty page for a moment while formulas load.
