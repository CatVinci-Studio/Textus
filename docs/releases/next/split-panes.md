### New

- Two documents at once, with the Split Panes plugin (Settings › Extensions › Plugins › Get plugins): Split Right (Ctrl+Alt+S, ⌘⌥S on a Mac) shows the document in front beside another tab, and Split Down puts one above the other. Drag the line between them to share the space, and double-click it to halve it again. A click in a pane brings its document to the front, and a tab you choose from the tab bar opens in that pane. Plugins can split the window too, through `textus.window.splitEditor`.
