### New

- The `textus` terminal command does more than open files: `textus help` lists its commands, and `list`, `read`, `search`, `new` and `move` work on your notes without opening a window. With Textus running, it also lists and runs palette commands, plugins' tools, themes, plugins and skills, and exports a document (`textus export notes.md --to pdf`). Settings › General may ask to install the command again once, so it can print.
