### Improved

- While Textus AI rewrites a passage, the sentence it has reached is tinted, and the tint moves on as it writes. Sentences it keeps as they were used to show nothing, so a light polish could sit still for seconds and then pop up a sentence.
- How finely you decide on a rewrite now follows what it did. A sentence or two changed: you decide sentence by sentence, as before. A paragraph rewritten through: it is one change, the old paragraph struck and the new one after it, instead of red and green pieces. Paragraphs joined or split: one change for each new paragraph, set against the paragraphs it came from. "Make this fewer paragraphs" used to come out as one change for everything.
- A margin card's ✓ reads "Take this change", since a change can now be a whole paragraph.

### Fixed

- A picture Textus AI redid now takes the old one's place in the text once it is made. It showed on its card, but the document kept the old picture and the card read "Writing…" for good.
- With Claude, a revision types into the document as it is written, rather than appearing a paragraph at a time.
- A rewrite that only joined paragraphs, with every sentence as it was, showed no change but joined them when accepted.
