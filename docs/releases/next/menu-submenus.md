### Fixed

- Submenus open in the floating view or Settings page their menu is in, keep going the way the menu turned at the window's edge, fade out instead of vanishing, and follow their row as the menu scrolls or the window changes size.
- Moving the pointer straight down a menu past a submenu opens the next row at once, and Escape or ← folds a submenu the pointer opened before a second Escape closes the menu.
- Menus play their closing fade again.
