### Improved

- Every colour in the window now comes from a variable an imported Typora theme can reach: the find bar's matches, the margin cards, the review bar, the switches and the shadows used to be fixed. The red of a delete button and the warning colour now follow the theme's page, as the other colours do.
- A theme can set the window's own colours on `:root` (`--sidebar-bg`, `--chrome-accent` and the rest, listed in the design guide), and they win over the ones Textus works out from its page.
