### Improved

- A phone no longer offers the same command twice: the outline is in the document's ⋯ and Settings in ☰, not also on the left icon bar; the sidebar and Textus AI are the buttons at the top, not also items in ☰; and an empty sidebar points to ☰ instead of repeating New File, Open and Open Folder.
- On a phone, the keyboard bar's ⋯ no longer repeats Insert Table (the bar has its own button) or Find (in the document's ⋯), and Settings › Extensions no longer repeats the theme store (in Appearance) or skills (in Memory & Skills).
- In a Textus AI tab, the + in the tab row starts a new conversation and says so; the second + inside the conversation is gone, History stays. Where there is no tab row (Zen, the tab bar hidden), the conversation keeps its own +.
- The same thing has the same name everywhere: **New File** (not New Tab or New Document), **Find…** (not Find & Replace), **Rich text** under **Editing view** in both the View menu and Settings, and **Textus AI** for the AI panel in the View menu.
- The Format menu has **Bold**, **Italic** and **Clear Formatting**, as the editor's right-click menu does.
- With no folder open, the sidebar shows one line and **Open Folder…**, instead of repeating the menu's New File and Open.
- Settings › Appearance › Window Layout's switch is now **Use the look of earlier versions**, so it no longer sounds like the Layout menu's **Classic** option.
- Wording is more consistent: Traditional Chinese says 資料夾 throughout, Japanese uses one word each for folder, document and export, **Show in Files** matches the Files tab, and the activity bar is called the same everywhere.

### Fixed

- An iPad's menu no longer lists what can't work there: the AI panel and AI guide where Textus AI isn't offered, Welcome and Tutorial, Report a Problem, and the Pandoc export formats.
- A tapped button at the top of a phone or tablet no longer keeps a grey box after the tap.
- On a Mac, Format's keys (⌘B, ⌘I, ⇧⌘H, ⌘K and the heading keys) no longer change the document while you are typing in another field, such as the find bar.
- On a phone with a keyboard, the Show Outline shortcut opens the outline menu instead of the Files list.
