### Fixed

- On macOS, pulling a document past its top or bottom (the rubber band) no longer swings back and forth or flickers: the swipe's momentum after the finger lifts no longer pulls the page out again, and formulas and other drawn blocks wait until it springs back instead of being drawn or let go mid-stretch.
- Source mode no longer stretches past its top or bottom: code stops at its ends; the rendered document keeps its give.
