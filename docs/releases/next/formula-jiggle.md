### Fixed

- A document with formulas no longer jiggles up and down by itself once scrolled to certain places, and on a Mac the pointer turns into the text cursor over it again: a formula three screens away was drawn and put away over and over, which on a Mac also held the pointer as an arrow.
- Formulas, diagrams and HTML far from the screen are put away exactly where they were drawn, so the lines around them no longer shift by a few pixels while you scroll.
- Very long documents scroll much more smoothly, and a document with many formulas or diagrams no longer shows blank stretches while scrolling: text far from the screen is drawn ahead in larger steps, and what you are reading stays still while the rest is drawn around it.
