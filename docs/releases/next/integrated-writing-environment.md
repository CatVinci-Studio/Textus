### New

- A new window: documents, Textus AI, Settings and plugin pages sit side by side in islands under one title bar that holds their tabs. Drag a tab to an island's edge to split it, or onto another island to join it; hold `Alt` to move a whole island.
- Textus AI opens in tabs of its own, each named Textus AI with its conversation's title on hover: **+** beside History starts a new conversation in a tab beside the one you have, and a conversation follows the window's folder.
- Plugins can use the network for the hosts they declare, show pictures from those hosts, go full screen (Slides) and hear when a picture you paste or drop is saved in the folder.
- Claude Code and Codex can answer in Textus AI as external agents, each through its own plugin.
- Each window keeps its own settings for its folder and its AI, and a reload brings back its plugin tabs and unsaved drafts.

### Improved

- Textus starts faster: the first load is about a third lighter, and the editor gets ready while the window is idle.
- Every panel fits its width: rows, headers, buttons and the composer step down or wrap instead of being cut off, in every language.
- One look across the app: solid surfaces, the same rows, badges, empty states and menus in Settings, the sidebar and plugin pages.
- Moving a file or folder to another disk copies it, keeps its links and conversations, and then moves the original to the Trash.
- Outline links keep maths in their anchors, and a nested `[[Note#A#B]]` link opens the same heading on hover as on click.

### Fixed

- The "Writing a continuation…" and "Checking grammar…" notes go away as soon as the result is in, instead of staying until they time out.
- The plugin store compares test versions by number, so a plugin made for a newer test build (Claude Code, Codex) installs again.
- A plugin list shows a page's own icon in place of the file icon.
