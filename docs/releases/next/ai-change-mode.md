### New

- Choose how Textus AI changes files in Settings › Textus AI: as revisions to review (the default) or written straight in. As revisions, a change to a file that isn't open waits in that file and shows as revisions when you open it; the file tree and its tab say how many wait there, the conversation lists each file, and a change that meets your own edits is listed instead of lost. A file Textus AI makes waits for you to keep it or discard it (to the Trash).
- Written straight in, the marks of what Textus AI changed stay after a restart.
