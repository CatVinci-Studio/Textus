### Improved

- The theme gallery shows each theme as it looks in your appearance, light or dark, and an enlarged preview pages to the other with ← and →. Our themes' descriptions are now in Chinese too.
- Opening Extensions › Create no longer flashes a blank panel first.
- A gallery theme opens its own page, like a plugin's: its whole description, both previews at full width, and Install or Use.
- Editor & Appearance › Text & Layout is shorter: the body text's font, size, letter spacing and line spacing fold under one Body text row that says whether you changed any.
