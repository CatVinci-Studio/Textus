### Improved

- The islands, their tabs and the sidebar move as one: opening the AI or a split grows the new island in from its edge while the others make room on the same frames, and nothing inside them is laid out again while they move.
- The AI, Settings and plugin pages keep their width when the window changes size; the documents take the rest.
- Only the tab in front of each island keeps an editor, as VS Code does: a tab you come back to opens at its text with its undo history, caret and reading place, and many open tabs no longer each hold a whole editor.
