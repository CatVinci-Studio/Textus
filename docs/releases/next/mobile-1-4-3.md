### Improved

- Phones and tablets: a long press on a file or folder in the sidebar opens its full menu, headed by its name; files can be shared from it.
- Phones and tablets: lists and colour settings open Textus's own menus and cards instead of the system pickers.
- Phones: the tab row scrolls with a swipe, All Tabs names drafts by their first line and marks the open one, and the ⋯ sheet offers the link the cursor is in.
- Phones and tablets: notices step aside while the keyboard is up, and the cursor stays above the format bar.
- iPhone and iPad: open documents, the tab order and the AI's folder come back after the app is reinstalled or updated.

### Fixed

- iPhone: the app sometimes started under the status bar and the home indicator until it was turned.
- Touch screens: a tap below or between tables puts the cursor where you tapped, instead of in the table's last cell or on a word iOS then replaced.
- Touch screens: the open tab's name is no longer cut short by its ×.
- Android: the back key, rotation, folding, system font size and dark mode keep your tabs and drafts.
- On iPad, the document that was in front when you left is in front again when you come back, not another one in the split.
