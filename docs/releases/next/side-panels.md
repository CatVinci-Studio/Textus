### Fixed

- Opens and closes the AI panel in one step, beside the page, whether or not the sidebar is open. On a Mac, opening it with the sidebar open used to squeeze the sidebar and the text and push them back, and the panel flashed as it settled. In a smaller window it also lay over the page with a shadow, unlike in full screen, and opening the sidebar afterwards changed it and played it in again.
- Keeps the window still when a swipe on the trackpad reaches the end of the sidebar or runs over the title bar, instead of the whole page bouncing on a Mac.
- Leaves the window as it is in Zen mode, which hides everything but the text and dims the other paragraphs, instead of also turning it fullscreen.
