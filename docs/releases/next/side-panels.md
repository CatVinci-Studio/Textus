### Fixed

- Slides the AI panel in beside the page without moving the rest of the window, whether or not the sidebar is open. On a Mac, opening it used to shift the sidebar and the text aside and snap them back as the slide ended. In a smaller window it also lay over the page with a shadow, unlike in full screen, and opening the sidebar afterwards changed it and played it in again; now it stays beside the page and keeps its width.
- Keeps the window still when a swipe on the trackpad reaches the end of the sidebar or runs over the title bar, instead of the whole page bouncing on a Mac. The document itself still gives at either end, short ones too.
- Leaves the window as it is in Zen mode, which hides everything but the text and dims the other paragraphs, instead of also turning it fullscreen.
- Keeps a Settings list inside the window when it is wider than its button, such as **Image model**'s, which ran past the window's right edge.
- Selects the document, or the text field you are in, with **Edit › Select All** on a Mac, instead of the whole window with the sidebar's notes when the caret was in neither.
