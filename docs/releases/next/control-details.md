### Improved

- Buttons with a shortcut show it in their hint as a small key, in the symbols of your system and following any shortcut you changed in Settings.
- Number fields (a font size, a line height) are one box: minus, the value and its unit, plus. The command palette's search has no box of its own and a long list fades at its edge.
- Dialog titles are larger than their message. Switches show their edge when off in dark mode, and checkboxes react under the pointer and draw their tick as they turn on.
- Settings no longer draws an empty box for a section with nothing in it, such as the web search section on Chat & Writing or an empty list of installed themes.
- A link's preview card no longer repeats the note's own title heading under its name. On a narrow phone a long tab name no longer runs under the AI button.
