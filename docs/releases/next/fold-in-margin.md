### Improved

- A heading's fold arrow now sits in the margin beside ⋮⋮ instead of inside the heading's text, so it no longer gets in the way of the caret, selection or typing at a heading's start; a folded heading keeps its arrow there.
- Mermaid diagrams and charts fold to one line with the same margin arrow as headings, embeds and plugin blocks, and stay folded when the document is opened again.
