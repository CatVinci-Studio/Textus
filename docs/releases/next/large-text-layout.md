### Improved

- At a large interface size (and a large system text size on a phone or tablet), Settings reads better: a switch stays beside its name however long it is, a value and its › are never split across lines, and at Extra Large and Largest each setting's name stands above its control. Menu rows wrap instead of cutting long names short.
- The document's margins stop growing at Large, so the larger steps give their room to the text; an iPad's sidebar no longer takes more than its share of the screen at the largest sizes.
