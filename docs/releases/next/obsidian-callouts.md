### New

- Obsidian callouts: every Obsidian type and alias (`[!info]`, `[!faq]`, `[!bug]`…) draws in colour, a title written after the marker replaces the label, `-` and `+` callouts fold from their chevron, and callouts nest. A type Textus doesn't know draws as a note. The file is saved exactly as written; folding changes only the view.
