### Improved

- On iPhone, the app runs to the screen's top edge: the title row sits under the status bar as one surface, and the document's first line starts below it.
- On iPhone, animations run at the screen's full 120 Hz, and themes keep their own fonts, Chinese and Japanese text included.
- On iPhone, Documents lists your files at once and fills in their dates and previews as they load; Open from Files is the folder button in its corner.

### Fixed

- If part of the window fails to draw, Textus shows what went wrong and a Reload button instead of a blank page.
- On iPhone and iPad, a tap no longer leaves a hover tip showing.
