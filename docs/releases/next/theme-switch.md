### Fixed

- A plugin's panel changes theme in the same frame as the window, instead of a frame later.
- Switching theme or light and dark changes every button, switch, checkbox and tab at once, instead of each fading over to its new colours at its own pace.
- An imported theme switches between its light and dark designs in the same frame as the rest of the window, and a built-in theme stays on the page until an imported one you pick has loaded, instead of the default look showing in between.
