### New

- Subscript and superscript as in Typora and Pandoc: `H~2~O` shows H₂O and `x^2^` shows x², and each forms as you type its closing `~` or `^`. Text with a space inside, such as `2^10 and 3^4`, stays as written. `~~text~~` is still strikethrough.

### Improved

- A single-tilde `~text~` shows as subscript, as Typora and Pandoc read it, instead of strikethrough. It was already saved with one tilde.
