### New

- Quick looks at what a link points to, with the Quick Preview plugin: rest the pointer on a link to another document - a Markdown link or a [[wikilink]] - and its opening, or the section the link names, shows in a card beside it. Pin keeps the card open, to move where you like while you write. With the Zotero plugin, resting on a citation such as [@darwin1859] shows what it cites. Plugins can fill such cards through `textus.editor.registerHoverProvider`.
