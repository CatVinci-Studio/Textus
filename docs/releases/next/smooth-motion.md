### Improved

- Glides instead of jumping when the page follows the caret past the window's edge, when Find goes to a match (now centred), and in typewriter mode. A scroll or a click stops it, and Reduce Motion turns it off.
- A row or column added to a table opens up, the cells beside it sliding over; a row or column dragged in a table, and a block dragged by its handle, glide into the place they are let go.
