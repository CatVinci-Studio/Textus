### New

- Brackets and quotes close themselves, as in Typora: typing `(`, `[`, `{`, a quote, or `（`, `【`, `「`, `“` from a Chinese or Japanese input method puts the closing partner in after the caret. Typing the closer steps over it, Backspace right after the opener removes both, and with text selected the pair goes around it. Nothing is added inside code, maths or a web address, before a word, or for an apostrophe as in "don't". Turn it off in Settings › Editor & Appearance › Auto-close brackets and quotes.

### Improved

- `_italic_` typed with underscores becomes italic and is saved as `_italic_`, instead of staying text saved as `\_italic\_`; `snake_case` and other underscores inside a word stay as they are.
- Tab and Shift+Tab in a table put the caret at the end of the next or previous cell's text, instead of selecting the whole cell, which the next key typed replaced.
- Shift+Enter in a table cell starts a new line in the cell, saved as `<br>` and shown as a line break again when the file is reopened. It used to do nothing.
