### Fixed

- Makes a link out of `[text](url)` written in any order: with the URL pasted between the parentheses, or with `[]()` typed first and filled in afterwards - it becomes a link once the caret leaves it. Brackets you escaped stay text.
- Keeps the words typed after a pasted link out of it.
- Makes a web address you type (`https://…`, `www.…`) a link as soon as you type a space or press Enter after it, as it already was after reopening the file.
