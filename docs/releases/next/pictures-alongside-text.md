### Improved

- Writes and illustrates in one go, on an empty page too: Textus AI asks for each picture first and goes on writing while it is made, and the picture's frame, softly moving and in its shape, stands in the text where the picture goes until it comes. The text and its pictures are one proposal; **Accept** says "Picture coming…" and waits until they have come, and a picture the service turned down says why in its place and is left out when you accept the text.
- Keeps what you haven't decided on: asking for a picture after a paragraph you haven't accepted yet puts the picture into that proposal, instead of replacing the paragraph, and a new request replaces an earlier proposal only when it is for the same place.
- Leaves a picture being redone as it is, dimmed, until the new one has come; only then are the two shown for you to choose.
- Says "Picture selected" when the selection is a picture, instead of counting its description's characters, and shows the picture itself to a model that can read images.
