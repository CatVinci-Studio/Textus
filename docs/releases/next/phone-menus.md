### Improved

- On a phone, a tap outside an open menu only closes it, as on iOS: it no longer also presses what is under it (the + beside the tabs).
- A phone menu opened while typing (the format bar's ⋯) puts the keyboard away and brings it back when it closes, unless the choice moved on to something else.
- On a phone, Back (and Escape) folds an open submenu before it closes the menu.
