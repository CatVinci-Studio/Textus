### New

- **Settings › Extensions** gathers what you add to Textus, a tab each: Themes (the theme gallery, and your own themes), Skills and MCP. Each one is a card with its switch.
- MCP servers give Textus AI more tools: a folder, a database, a search, anything with an MCP server. Add one by the command that starts it or by its URL, try it before saving to see its tools, and turn it on; Textus starts it in the background, and Textus AI may call its tools while it helps you, each call shown in the conversation. arXiv (search and read papers), ARES (find papers and their BibTeX by DOI) and Context7 (up-to-date documentation for programming libraries) are ready to add, and none of them needs an account or a key. The command is found the way your terminal finds it, so npx and uvx work from the Dock too.

### Improved

- Skills are optional: a new installation starts with five on - Proofread, Tighten, Expand, Summarize and Translate - and you add the others in **Settings › Extensions › Skills**. If you used Textus before, every skill you had stays on. Your own skills, Customize… and Reset are there too; **Settings › Textus AI** points to them.
