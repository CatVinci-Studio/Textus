### Improved

- Everything you type for now opens in one panel: `/` commands, `:emoji`, `[[` notes and every plugin's suggestions show together, grouped by where they come from. It opens with the key you press, filters 10,000 notes as fast as you type, keeps the row you chose while slower plugins answer, and owns the arrow keys, Enter, Tab and Esc while open - even with Vim or Emacs keys on.
- `[[` lists the notes in your folder, and typing `[[` over selected words turns them into a link. A note you pick is written as a link that stays one after saving.
