### Improved

- **View › Mode** holds the four ways to see a document - Default, Source, Source and Preview, and Reading Mode (Read Only before) - one at a time, with Track My Changes, Zen and Typewriter beside them to add on. Their keys work as before: pressing one again goes back to Default.
- The **Plugins** menu puts a plugin's commands in a submenu of its name when it has several.
- The sidebar's tabs can be **dragged** into the order you like.
