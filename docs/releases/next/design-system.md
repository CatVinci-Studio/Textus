### Improved

- One look across the app: every panel, card and dialog uses the same three corner sizes, every button, field and switch is a capsule, and text sizes follow one scale - iOS's on the iPhone and iPad.
- Themes recolour the whole window, the built-in ones too: the sidebar, Settings, menus and the AI panel take the theme's colours. Typora themes' own sidebar colours are used.
- On the iPhone the AI composer's mode is a compact picker beside its tools, and switches sit in proportion to their labels.
