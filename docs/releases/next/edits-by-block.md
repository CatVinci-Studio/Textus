### Improved

- Makes Textus AI's revisions show up at once: it names the paragraph it changes instead of copying it out first, so a rewrite starts typing into the document as soon as its reply is written - a rewrite of a whole document on a ChatGPT subscription went from about 10 seconds of nothing to a fifth of a second. It also costs less, since the copied passage is no longer sent back, and long documents gain the most: a section read from a long document is changed the same way. Each changed paragraph still gets its own card.
- Shows a picture's frame - in its place and in its shape - while Textus AI is still describing it, rather than once the whole description has come; redoing a picture starts on the old one straight away.
- Keeps a line under the reply saying what Textus AI is doing - "Writing the edit…", "Generating an image…" or "Thinking…" - whenever nothing else moves, so a long edit or picture no longer looks stuck.
