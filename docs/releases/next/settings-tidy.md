### Improved

- Settings are easier to scan:
  - **General**: what opens at launch, after the last document closes and from the tab row's + is now its own group, **Opening and closing**. The proxy moved into **Application**.
  - **Appearance** has a **Text** group: the document's font and size, text width, first-line indent and smooth caret moved here from Editing. **Use the classic look** sets the classic layout, hides the activity bar and puts the title bar on its own row; turned off, it puts back what you had. A theme is added from a CSS file in **Installed themes › ⋯ › Import CSS…**, and your own CSS is written in **Installed themes › ⋯ › Custom CSS…**.
  - **Editing › Text** is now **Editing › Document** (its text settings moved to **Appearance › Text**): the empty-line hint ("Type / to insert") and the block handles (⋮⋮) are always on, with no switch; the plain-text copy and paste menu items moved to **View**. Markdown is split into **Display** and **Typing**; colour swatches and the ** and ` pairs left Advanced.
  - **Editing › Export** is split into **PDF** and **Word and Pandoc**. Pandoc arguments take one row: the format, then its arguments.
  - **Textus AI**: the pages are now called **Chat & writing** and **Memory & skills**. A list's "?" follows its title.
  - **Privacy**: the button that deletes recovery drafts says **Delete**.
