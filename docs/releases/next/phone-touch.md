### Improved

- A phone turned on its side keeps the phone layout, instead of switching to the desktop's, clear of the camera cutout and the far corner.
- On a phone and a touch iPad, the rail and Settings icons, the bar's buttons, the tab row's +, the file tree's tools and rows, list rows and menu rows each take a full fingertip (44pt); the phone's small bar and rail icons are drawn a little larger.
- A phone's menus - the document's ⋯, its outline, a long press on a file, a select - are now Textus's own menu, the one ☰ and the desktop use, opening where they were asked for, instead of a sheet from the bottom; **Save changes?** is the desktop's dialog.
- A phone no longer offers the same command twice: the outline is in the document's ⋯ and Settings in ☰, not also on the rail; the sidebar and Textus AI are the bar's buttons, not also rows in ☰; and an empty sidebar points to ☰ instead of repeating New, Open and Open Folder.
- On a phone, the keyboard bar's More no longer repeats Insert Table (the bar's own button) or Find (in ⋯), and Settings › Extensions no longer repeats the theme store (in Appearance) or skills (in Memory & Skills).
- An iPad's menu no longer lists what can't work there: the AI panel and AI guide where Textus AI isn't offered, Welcome and Tutorial, Report a Problem, and the Pandoc export formats.
