### Improved

- A phone turned on its side keeps the phone layout, instead of switching to the desktop's.
- On a phone and a touch iPad, the rail and Settings icons, the bar's buttons, the tab's × and +, the file tree's tools and rows, and menu rows each take a full fingertip (44pt); the phone's small bar and rail icons are drawn a little larger.
- A phone's menus - the document's ⋯, its outline, a long press on a file, a select - are now Textus's own menu, the one ☰ and the desktop use, opening where they were asked for, instead of a sheet from the bottom; **Save changes?** is the desktop's dialog.
