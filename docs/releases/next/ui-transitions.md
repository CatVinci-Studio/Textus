### Improved

- Switching the sidebar between Files, Outline, Search and Plugins slides the new panel in from its side of the activity bar as it fades in, so you can see which way you went. With Reduce Motion on, it switches at once.
- Changing the theme, or between light, dark and following the system, in Settings fades the whole window across in a moment instead of snapping. On older macOS and Windows versions, and with Reduce Motion on, it switches at once as before.
